Project 0

Project 0 is a space to be intentional about what you take in, and turn more of it into something new. I believe consuming and creating work best in a loop: the right input feeds the next thing you build, and building sharpens what you go looking for next.


What does it involve?

Each month, I host 3 sessions around a theme:

  1. Create: learn by building your own project
  2. Consume: discuss a curated list of mixed media
  3. Collect: swap and share ideas or items
